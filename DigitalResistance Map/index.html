Here in New York, larger towering structures serve as a device or a reminder of our scale in relation to this world or idea of forever. Looking upward, head tilted towards the monumental. We can become so preoccupied with what exists above and beyond us that we fail to recognize the intimacy of what is around us, what is within reach or beneath our feet. We are constantly in relationship with the spaces, people and objects that surround us. Our bodies are being held and transfigured. Maybe there is as much joy and wonder in paying attention. Maybe we ignore what is close as an act of self-preservation. To look up means we can look away. Paying attention requires a sticky discomfort. To confront the conditions of our immediate environment and feel our proximity, our complicity and to really look at it, to truly see it, indifference becomes harder to sustain.
